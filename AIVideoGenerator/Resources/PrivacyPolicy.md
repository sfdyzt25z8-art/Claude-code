# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

## Overview

AI Video Generator ("the App") lets you generate videos and thumbnails from text prompts using third-party and first-party AI models. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Data We Collect

- **Account information**: name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email.
- **Generated content**: prompts you submit and the resulting videos/thumbnails, stored on your device and, if you enable cloud sync, in our cloud storage.
- **Usage data**: crash logs and feature usage, used only to improve reliability and performance.

## How We Use Your Data

- To generate the content you request via your selected AI provider.
- To sync your projects across your devices when cloud sync is enabled.
- To enforce subscription limits and prevent abuse (rate limiting, content moderation).

## Third-Party AI Providers

Prompts and generation parameters are sent to the AI provider you select in Settings ▸ AI Provider Selection in order to fulfill your request. We do not share your account credentials with these providers.

## Content Moderation

All generation requests pass through automated content moderation before being submitted to an AI provider, to prevent generation of content that violates our Terms of Service or Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

## Your Choices

- You may disable cloud sync at any time in Settings.
- You may delete your account and all associated content at any time.
- You may report content that appears to violate these policies from Settings ▸ Report Content.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
