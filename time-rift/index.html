HOW TO PLAY

THE RIFT MECHANIC — every time you restart a level (by dying or pressing R), your run is saved as a translucent time clone that repeats your exact actions from the start. Use your past selves to hold switches, distract enemies, or block hazards while you take a different path.

KEYBOARD

  • A / D — move left / right
  • W / SPACE — jump
  • J — attack
  • E — interact
  • R — restart level (spawns a clone)
  • ESC — pause

OBJECTS

  • Time Switch — press with weight, self or clone
  • Time Door — opens once its switch(es) are active
  • Time Crystal — restores health, marks a checkpoint
  • Rift Portal — reach it to finish the level
  • Time Barrier — only solid part of the time
